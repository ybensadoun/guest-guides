Privacy

Your data, in plain words.

What we keep about you, why, who helps us, and how to have it deleted.

Last updated 29 October 2026

Who we are

Guest Guide Maker makes guest guides for holiday homes. It is run by Yohann Bensadoun, who decides what happens to your data (the "controller", in the words of the GDPR).

Questions and requests: yohann.bensadoun@gmail.com

What we keep, and why

  • Your account. Your email address, and your password (stored scrambled: nobody can read it) or, if you sign in with Google, the name and email address Google shares with us. So you can sign in and come back.
  • Your house and your guide. The listing link you give us and what that public listing page shows (title, photos, house rules, rating), your answers (for example Wi-Fi, arrival, rubbish days, your phone number if you add it), the photos you upload and the places you choose. To build your guide and keep it up to date.
  • A little about your device. Your browser and screen size, when you create an account or send us a note. To fix problems.

We do not sell your data, we show no ads and we use no trackers.

What your guests see

Your guide is public to anyone who has its link. Everything you put in it can be read by them, Wi-Fi included. Guides do not record who opens them.

Who helps us

  • SupabaseIreland, EUAccounts, the database and your photos.
  • NetlifyUSAHosts this site and the guides, and passes on sign-up notices and your notes to us.
  • HetznerGermany, EUThe server that builds the guides.
  • Anthropic (Claude)USAReads your listing page and writes the first version of your guide, translates it and looks up places near the house. It never gets your email address, your password or your Wi-Fi details.
  • GoogleUSAPlaces near the house, Sign in with Google if you choose it, and the fonts of this site.
  • ResendUSASends us, not you, a monthly email report about the guides.
  • OpenFreeMap and Photon (Komoot)MapsThe maps in your guide and searching on them.

Where data leaves the EU, these companies' own data processing terms apply, with the safeguards the GDPR requires.

On your device

This site keeps your answers, the photos not sent yet and your sign-in in your browser's storage, so nothing is lost if you close the page. No advertising cookies.

How long, and your rights

We keep your data while you have an account. Ask us and we delete your account, your guides and your photos within 30 days.

You can also ask to see, correct or export your data, or object to how we use it: write to yohann.bensadoun@gmail.com. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Spanish data protection authority (aepd.es) or to the one where you live.
