# Guest Guides: engine and Guest Guide Maker (PRODUCT)

Read first: `../CLAUDE.md` (shared rules: publishing, privacy, languages, curation, UX), then `../PROJECTS.md` and `../CHANGES.md`.
This repo is PUBLIC (ybensadoun/guest-guides). No keys, host data, Wi-Fi or business plans in it. Business backlog: `../BACKLOG.md` (outside the repo).

## What lives here
| Folder | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `engine/` | Shared guide code: Vite + vanilla JS PWA, MapLibre with OpenFreeMap, 6 languages | A change here rebuilds every guide. Never site-specific code. |
| `sites/<slug>/` | One folder of data per guide | `ca-la-kimberly` is the real guide (its own CLAUDE.md, and the source of truth for the section structure). All others are local tests: never publish (see `sites/README.md`). |
| `onboarding-site/` | Guest Guide Maker: sign-in, then one set of steps that first collects the answers and, once the guide exists, edits the guide itself (`src/editor.js`, drafts and Publish changes: `docs/plan-single-editor.md`). Live at porta-guides | Hosts see "Guest Guide Maker"; "Porta" is only an internal name. Details in its README. |
| `worker/` | Builds guides that hosts publish. Runs on the server (Hetzner `guide-worker`, Docker) since 28 Oct 2026, not on the Mac | See `worker/README.md`. Secrets: `/etc/guide-worker.env` on the server, `worker/.env.local` on the Mac for tests. |
| `docs/` | Rules and plans | TYPOGRAPHY, CURATION, UX-GUIDELINES, plan-accounts-and-publishing, runbooks, learnings |

## Product rules
- Every product guide has exactly Ca La Kimberly's sections: read `sites/ca-la-kimberly/site.json` and `content/sections.json`, never from memory. When the guide changes its structure, the product follows (CHANGES.md). One addition (Yohann, 29 Oct 2026): Nightlife, after Things to do, is part of the design, off unless the host switches it on, the host's own list like Eat and drink.
- Ca La Kimberly is a guide like any other (Yohann, 10 Nov 2026): no exception for it in the code or its data, and everything in it must be something the maker can produce from a host's answers and the editor.
- Quick for the host (Yohann, 10 Nov 2026): a host gives the least possible (a link, a name, a few words only they know) and everything else is filled in for them and shown to look at once, never an empty form to complete. A new field in the builder or the editor needs a source that fills it; what cannot be filled stays out of the host's way. Sources must respect each service's terms (`../CLAUDE.md`).
- Stable and credited (Yohann, 10 Nov 2026): what a guide shows must be ours to keep (the host's own words and pins, open data whose licence allows it), so a guest finds the same guide whatever Google or our server did that week; what has to be renewed from a service is the exception and never simply disappears. Every source is credited where its terms ask, built at the same time as its use ("Google Maps" beside Google's content, OpenStreetMap on the map, GeoNames in the guide's credits).
- Nothing waits for the host (Yohann, 12 Nov 2026): work in the background (the research, a reading) starts as early as it can and needs none of the host's answers, so that everything is filled when the host reaches a step; the host's answers then act at once on what was found (a cut, an order), without a new search. Thinking states, above all a wait for an AI call, are hidden as much as possible.
- Around you, as hosts see it (builder and editor, 29 Oct 2026): Attractions (things to do), Eat and drink, Local markets, Local events, Nightlife, each step saying what goes in it and what does not (no restaurant in Attractions). Guests keep the engine's names. Kids and dogs are filters inside Attractions (and later Local events), not sections.
- Research: Spain only for now; at least 4 markets, nearest first; flag hours that are not confirmed; every place passes the existence rule before a host guide goes live.
- Homes only, Spain first (Yohann, 11 Nov 2026): resorts and the Philippines were explored in a separate project and closed. Before adapting the maker to another kind of place or country, read `docs/reference-types-and-countries.md` (the type x country model, what changed, the archived code).
- Things to do hold only what the host chose (Yohann, 30 Oct 2026): swiped yes or typed, one list, no badge. Researched places are shown first: cards in the deck after sign-up, then in the editor. Never add them to a guide without the host seeing them.
- Listing reading (`/api/listing`): copy what the page states, never guess; only tagline and welcome text are written by Claude and marked as such ("Suggested for you: please check": hosts are not shown Claude's name, Yohann, 11 Nov 2026).
- The onboarding and editor follow the same UX, typography and no em-dash rules as the guides.
- Desktop and phone (Yohann, 11 Nov 2026): most hosts use the maker on a desktop computer, most guests open the guide on a phone. The maker (builder and editor) is designed and checked at both widths, about 1280 px with the phone preview beside it and 360 px, with pictures of both before saying something is done; the guide stays mobile first.
- Phase 2 safety (before real hosts): see `../GITHUB-SETUP.md` (separate code from customer data, GDPR, keys per purpose).

## Engine conventions
- No framework. Views in `engine/src/views/`, router in `main.js`, styles in `engine/src/styles/app.css` (later blocks override earlier ones), icons in `engine/src/ui.js`.
- Modules (13 Nov 2026): a part of the guide kept as one folder, `engine/src/modules/<kind>/`, with a `README.md` that says its rules (who decided each and when), its data, its look and its Home widget; `page(V)` draws its page, `widget(now)` gives what Home's tile is made of, and `modules/index.js` lists them. Rubbish (`garbage`) is the first; the other sections move one at a time. Read a module's README before changing how it behaves and write the changed rule there. The reference for a module's look is Ca La Kimberly: compare pixel for pixel before and after.
- UI strings: `engine/src/i18n/<lang>.json`, all six languages, same keys. `typo()` in `i18n.js` adds French spaces at render time; the build lints the other typography rules.
- Content files carry a `_doc` field describing their schema: read it before editing.
- Scroll effects: driven by a `--k` value from a requestAnimationFrame scroll handler, no CSS transitions. Back arrow uses in-app history depth (`history.state.d`).
- Home tiles: icon top-left, shape in the top-right corner only, full-width title, arrow on its own row bottom-right. Titles scale with tile width (container units, 19 px minimum).
- Test with Playwright screenshots at 360 px in de and fr before saying something is done.
