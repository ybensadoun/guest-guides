# Ca La Kimberly: design direction "Bauhaus summer"

## Who uses it
The listing is "Ca La Kimberly Pool House": a whole house for up to 8, 4 bedrooms, a pool, a barbecue, mountain views and a smart lock. Reviews highlight the pool, the outdoor spaces and that it suits families. Guests are families and groups of friends, mostly 30+, from Spain, France, Germany, the Netherlands and the UK. They come by car and stay several days.

These are assumptions drawn from the listing and our conversations, not from real interviews. Validate them with a few past guests.

| Moment | What they need | What the app does |
|---|---|---|
| The first evening | "What do we do with the rubbish tonight?" | The Garbage tile shows tonight's bins as pictures, before any text |
| Breakfast | "Is there a market this morning, and is it still open?" | The Markets tile shows today's count, and the "Markets today" card shows live status |
| By the pool, one phone passed around | Something readable in sunlight, at arm's length | Big type (17px body, 23 to 36px titles), solid colours, no pastels |
| A problem | Emergency contacts, fast | A red tile, always in the same place |
| Phone in another language | Understand without reading much | Pictograms and colour do most of the work, in 6 languages |

## Principles
1. **One question per tile.** Each tile answers one thing. Live tiles end with an arrow; tiles still to come carry a "Soon" sticker.
2. **Pictures over words, but only when they add something.** The bins are drawn as bins. Section titles carry no icons. The top bar is plain.
3. **Big type.** The minimum is 14px, body text is 17px, and titles are 23 to 36px. The font is Jost, a geometric face in the Futura family with Bauhaus roots, and it is easy to read.
4. **Flat primary colours.** Blue, red, yellow and black, plus orange and green, so no two tiles share a colour. Paper white is the background.
5. **One shape per tile.** A circle, a square, a triangle or a half-circle, each in a contrasting palette colour, placed away from the text. This is what makes it feel like a poster and not like a dashboard.
6. **Calm chrome.** The white top and bottom bars have a black rule. The colour is saved for the content.

## Palette (all text pairs checked for WCAG AA)
| Name | Hex | Text on it | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper (background) | #F3EEE3 | Ink | 16.2 |
| Ink | #121212 | White | 18.7 |
| Blue | #1D4FA0 | White | 7.8 |
| Red | #D52B1E | White | 5.0 |
| Yellow | #F5C400 | Ink | 11.4 |
| Orange | #EC6A1C | Ink | 5.9 |
| Green | #0E7C46 | White | 5.3 |
| Muted text | #4A4A4A | on Paper | 7.7 |

Red text on Paper is 4.3, so red is never used for small text.
The bin colours stay close to the real bins (brown, yellow, blue, grey, red, green) so guests recognise them outside.

## Navigation
Four tabs: Home, Markets, Garbage, Map. Every other section is one tap away on Home, so the "More" tab is gone. The active tab sits on a yellow circle.
