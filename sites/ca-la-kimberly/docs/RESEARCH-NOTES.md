# Research notes: markets around Can Gordei

Compiled in a Claude chat in October 2026. Verify anything flagged before relying on it.

## Area covered
Baix Penedès, Alt Camp, Tarragonès and Alt Penedès. Distances are rough road km from the house at Can Gordei (41.2643, 1.4748), estimated from straight-line distance × about 1.3.

## Main sources
- **mercatsetmanal.cat:** listings fed by town halls. Confirmed La Bisbal (Wed 8:30 to 13:30), Banyeres (Wed), Bonastre (Tue 9 to 13), Llorenç (Mon 8 to 14), Albinyana (Sun), L'Arboç (Tue).
- **mercadillosemanal.com and mercadillos.org:** source for most stall counts and product lists. Treat counts as approximate.
  - Creixell 150, Bonavista 400, Coma-ruga 80, El Vendrell 95, Cunit 85, Torredembarra 100, Calafell 70, Roda de Berà 60, Corsini 70, Sant Quadrat 20, Catedral 24 to 50, Valls 100, Constantí 30, Alcover 25, L'Arboç 40, Llorenç 20, Bisbal 10, La Papiola 70 + 70, Els Monjos 200.
- **El Vendrell town hall and tourism site:**
  - Mercat Municipal: Mon to Sat 8 to 14, plus Tue, Fri and Sat 17:30 to 20:30.
  - Friday market layout.
  - Antiques market on the 2nd Saturday, 9 to 15.
  - Racó de l'Art crafts.
- **Vilafranca town hall and tourism site:**
  - Saturday market: 210 stalls, food 8 to 14, other stalls 9 to 14.
  - Daily veg market at Pl. Sant Joan.
  - Tuesday Espirall market.
  - Crafts, antiques and painters market on the 2nd Sunday, October to May.
- **Calafell town hall:** Segur de Calafell swap and second-hand market (occasional Sundays, ~200 private sellers).
- **Torredembarra town hall:** eco swap market, twice a year.
- **Tarragona Mercat Central:** Mon to Sat 8:30 to 21:00.

## Decisions already made
- "Albinyana flea market" is really La Papiola, next to the C-51, which has two markets on the same site:
  - Saturday: second-hand, ~70 stalls.
  - Sunday: general market, ~70 stalls.
  
  The old separate "Albinyana village" Sunday entry was merged into the Sunday one.
- Bonavista is classed as a general street market (huge, bargain-oriented), not a flea market.
- Covered municipal markets are excluded from the home page "N today" count, because they open every weekday.
- Categories: covered, street, flea, antiques (antiques, brocante, crafts), events (swap markets and yearly fairs).

## Uncertain, keep flagged
- Mercat de Calafell (covered): hours listed online as 9 to 21 daily, probably the bars inside. Food stalls are likely mornings only.
- Vilafranca, Mercat de Sant Salvador: hours not found, set to weekday mornings.
- Valls antiques market: one source says every Saturday, another says the 1st Saturday. Set to the 1st Saturday.
- Sant Jaume dels Domenys: information from 2021, only 2 or 3 stalls.
- Bellvei (Ca Susi second-hand, Sundays): a single source.
- Yearly fairs (Santa Teresa in El Vendrell, Santa Úrsula in Valls, the Tarragona medieval market, Sarroca Medieval): exact 2026 dates not confirmed.
- Coordinates are approximate for most village markets; La Papiola, Creixell and Santa Oliva come from directory map links.

## Market names (updated October 2026)
Each market has `name: {ca, es, en, fr, de, nl}`, shown as the card title. All names follow one pattern: place + type.
- EN: place first, "El Vendrell covered market", "La Bisbal Wednesday market".
- ES / FR / CA: type first, "Mercado Municipal de El Vendrell", "Halles d'El Vendrell", "Mercat Municipal del Vendrell".
- DE / NL (added October 2026): type then place, no preposition, "Markthalle El Vendrell", "Mittwochsmarkt La Bisbal", "Overdekte markt El Vendrell", "Woensdagmarkt La Bisbal".
- Weekly markets are named after their day, which is how people refer to them locally.
- A local name goes in brackets when it helps find it on the spot: (Racó de l'Art), (Ca Susi), (Corsini), (Sant Quadrat), (L'Espirall), (Sant Salvador).
- The place is a short form of the town (La Bisbal, Els Monjos, Sant Sadurní) or the site (La Papiola, Bonavista). The full town and comarca show on the card.

## Map grouping (added October 2026)
- Markets are grouped on the map by `town`, or by `group` when set.
- Bonavista has `group: "Bonavista"`: it is in Tarragona municipality but about 5 km from the centre, so grouping it with the central Tarragona markets would stop the town zoom from reaching street level.
- At the widest zoom, towns whose pins would overlap on screen share a cluster pin (for example "El Vendrell +6"). Tapping it zooms in until they separate.

## Opening hours research (October 2026)
- Santa Oliva Saturday market: REMOVED 16 Oct 2026. Guests found no market there. Only source was Diari de Tarragona, 22 March 2021 (restart after closing in 2018); the town hall site lists no current weekly market. Do not re-add without a recent source.

- La Pobla de Montornès Saturday market: Avinguda de Catalunya and its car park (town hall, April 2026). 8:30 to 13:30 (mercatsetmanal.cat). The car park is closed until 16:00 for set-up and take-down.
- Sant Jaume dels Domenys: Thursday mornings (town hall, 2021). No hours published anywhere; set to 8:30 to 13:30 like the similar small village markets nearby (La Bisbal, Banyeres). Still flagged "older".
- Vilafranca vegetable market (Pl. Sant Joan): Monday to Friday 8:00 to 13:30 (mercatsetmanal.cat). Vilafranca tourism only says "every working morning". On Saturdays the big market covers the centre.
- Vilafranca Sant Salvador covered market: C/ Francesc Macià 1, Monday to Saturday 9 to 21, Sunday closed (Diputació de Barcelona market data, dadesmercatsdiba.cat). Like Calafell, some fresh stalls may close earlier. "check" flag removed.
- No market uses "mornings" any more. The app still supports `hours_approximate_morning` ("This morning") if one is needed later.
