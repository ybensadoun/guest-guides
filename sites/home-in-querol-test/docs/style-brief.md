- In their words: # Design Direction: Contemporary Catalan Rustic Luxury

Design a modern, premium website inspired by an authentic Catalan masia: a traditional rural farmhouse with centuries of history, surrounded by Mediterranean nature.

## Creative vision
Combine the authenticity of an old Catalan farmhouse with the understated sophistication of a boutique luxury hotel. The experience should feel warm, peaceful, tactile, exclusive and deeply connected to its surroundings.

The luxury should come from the quality of the photography, the elegance of the typography, the use of space and the attention to detail — never from flashy visual effects or excessive decoration.

## Colour palette
- Olive green: #565F45 — brand identity and selected interface elements.
- Terracotta: #B65C3A — primary calls to action and occasional accents.
- Warm stone: #D9C9B1 — secondary surfaces and dividers.
- Soft sand: #F2EADF — primary background.
- Charcoal: #2E2D29 — typography and icons.

## Typography
Use an elegant editorial serif font for headlines, such as Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond, paired with a clean, highly legible sans-serif such as Inter for body copy, navigation, forms and buttons.

Use generous spacing, refined text hierarchy and restrained letter spacing for small uppercase labels.

## Photography and imagery
Prioritise large, beautiful, authentic photographs of:
- Traditional Catalan stone architecture and terracotta rooftops.
- Olive groves, cypress trees, Mediterranean landscapes and golden-hour sunlight.
- Exposed wooden beams, stone walls, natural linen and handmade ceramics.
- Carefully styled but lived-in interiors.
- Outdoor terraces, local produce and slow Mediterranean living.

Use warm, natural colours and realistic textures. Avoid oversaturated images, artificial HDR, generic luxury-hotel photography and overly polished CGI.

## Interface and layout
Create a clean, responsive, editorial-style experience with generous whitespace, asymmetrical image layouts, large immersive photography and minimal navigation.

Use subtle rounded corners, fine borders, discreet hover effects and smooth, restrained transitions. Buttons should be elegant and understated, with clear contrast and excellent usability.

Prioritise accessibility, readability, mobile responsiveness and intuitive navigation.

## Brand personality
Authentic, timeless, Mediterranean, welcoming, refined, peaceful and quietly luxurious.

## Avoid
Gold gradients, shiny metallic effects, black-and-gold luxury clichés, excessive shadows, glassmorphism, generic corporate layouts, rustic clichés, decorative clutter and excessive use of terracotta.

The final result should feel like an independent design-led boutique retreat in the Catalan countryside: heritage and craftsmanship expressed through a contemporary digital experience.
